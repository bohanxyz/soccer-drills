Pressure Turn

The player receives from Wall A and opens up for a half-turn. When the ball hits Wall A, the iPad hears it, waits a random beat, then flashes a pressure cue for about half a second. The cue tells the player what to do with the ball. A late head check misses it.

Cues

Tap a cue to turn it on or off. Each flash picks one of the cues that are on.
